Why Fresh For Less Started

Morales Produce Co. has been hauling and distributing produce for over 20 years. Three years ago, we opened our Fresh For Less retail storefront right here in Bernalillo so local families could buy produce at the exact same quality and low prices that our commercial wholesale accounts receive.

Why Our Prices Are Lower

Behind the retail store, Morales Produce supplies roughly 100 restaurants, markets, paleterías, and cafés across the region. Because we buy and haul produce by the full truckload, our retail store gets the exact same fresh, top-tier product without paying distributor markup. We pass those savings straight to you.

Fast, Owner-Direct Service

Jose Luis "JL" Morales Jr. knows his growers, his drivers, and his customers personally. There are no corporate buyers or middlemen deciding what sits on our shelves.

Horticulture Expertise Backing Our Quality

JL's wife, Lu, works in the business and holds a master's degree in horticulture from Texas A&M. That scientific background gives our family a direct eye for crop quality, seed varieties, soil health, and chile ripeness.
