Our Signature

Red or Green? — Roasted by the Morales Family

Learn about our chile →

Chile is what we are known for. In season, our roasters are turning daily with fresh New Mexico green chile roasted right outside the door.

Inside on the floor and porch, we keep sun-dried red chile pods by the sack, freshly tied red chile ristras, and pure ground red chile powder ready for authentic enchiladas, carne adovada, and posole. Pure flavor, direct from the fields.

Why Our Prices Stay Low

We haul direct by the truckload.

Behind our Bernalillo store, Morales Produce supplies roughly 100 restaurants, markets, and cafés. Buying and hauling by the semi-truckload is how we bring fresh produce, watermelons, citrus, and chile directly to local families without distributor markup.
