Tradition & Flavor

Sun-Dried Ristras, Pods & Ground Powder

From hand-tied red chile ristras hanging along our eaves to sun-dried pods stacked by the sack, we provide true unadulterated New Mexico chile.

Our ground red chile powder is pure chile—no salt, no dyes, no anti-caking additives.

Seasonal Harvest (August – October)

Green Chile Roasting Outside the Store

During harvest season and Balloon Fiesta, our commercial roasting drums run right in front of the store. We roast sacks of fresh New Mexico green chile to order so you get the true blistered char and sweet roasted heat ready to peel and freeze for the winter.

Backed by Real Crop Knowledge

JL's wife, Lu, holds a master's in horticulture from Texas A&M. Her training in plant science, crop genetics, and post-harvest quality helps our family evaluate every sack of chile we source to ensure authentic texture, heat, and pure New Mexico flavor.
